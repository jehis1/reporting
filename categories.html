Monitored Infrastructure Types

The platform handles multiple categories of municipal defects, automatically routing them to the correct administrative body based on spatial data and jurisdictional policies[cite: 17, 18].

Potholes & Roads

Covers surface damage, cracks, and major wear on urban pathways. Responsibilities are divided between county transport departments and national authorities like KURA and KeNHA[cite: 17].

Street Lighting

Addresses unlit corridors and broken poles that impact nocturnal public safety and security in urban neighborhoods[cite: 17].

Drainage & Flooding

Monitors blocked drainage channels, overflowing culverts, and stormwater management issues exacerbated by seasonal heavy rains[cite: 17].

Traffic Signage

Tracks missing, obscured, or damaged warning signs and road markings essential for preventing traffic accidents[cite: 17].

Routing Policy

Every submitted report is analyzed by the prioritization engine to determine the responsible authority before dispatching[cite: 17, 18].
