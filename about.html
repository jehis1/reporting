Bridging Information Asymmetry in Urban Infrastructure

The state of public infrastructure in Kenyan urban areas—depicted by broken roads, dysfunctional streetlights, and inadequate road signs—presents persistent management challenges[cite: 17]. City authorities often lack a continuous record of defects, leading to purely reactive maintenance[cite: 17].

Report It provides a robust digital framework enabling citizens to log infrastructure degradation with multimedia evidence and precise GPS coordinates[cite: 17]. The integrated prioritization engine processes these submissions based on severity, safety risks, and reporting frequency to direct limited municipal budgets efficiently[cite: 17].

"Transforming civic technologies from simple communication loops into active instruments of municipal responsiveness[cite: 17]."
